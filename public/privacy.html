Privacy notice

What Granum collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Applies to the Granum website, the download page and the Granum app.

Your datasets

Granum runs on your computer. Your images, labels, models and projects stay there. We never receive them.

What we collect

  • On the download page: your email address, and your name and company if you give them, with the operating system you chose and the time.
  • When you buy a plan or ask for a quote: your email address, name and company, the plan, the number of computers and your message.
  • When you sign in inside the app: your email address, a one-time sign-in code, an identifier for the computer (a one-way hash of the operating system's install ID, not a hardware serial number), and the app version.
  • While the licence renews: the licence key, which contains the above, and the time of each renewal.

Why

To send you the installer, to answer purchase and quote requests, to run your free trial and plans (one trial per person and per computer, and the number of computers on a plan), to email sign-in codes, and to reply when you write to us. We do not sell or share this data, and we do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.

Where it is kept

On our hosting provider (Vercel) and database provider (Neon), and our email provider for sign-in codes. Records are kept while you have a trial or plan with us, and deleted on request after that.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, for it to be corrected, or for it to be deleted. Deleting your record ends any trial or plan tied to it.
