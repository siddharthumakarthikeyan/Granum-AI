Privacy notice

What Granum collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Applies to the Granum website, the download page and the Granum app.

Your datasets

Local-only workflows keep your images, labels, models and projects where you configure them. Optional cloud storage, shared-service access and material you explicitly send for support cross that boundary. The current alpha adds no dataset telemetry.

What we collect

  • On the download page: your email address, and your name and company if you give them, with the operating system you chose and the time.
  • When you request a pilot: your email address, name and company, the requested pilot category, the number of computers and your message.
  • Only for explicit activation in older or separately licensed builds: email, one-time sign-in code, a hashed machine identifier and app version. The current unrestricted alpha does not require this activation.
  • Only when those licensed builds renew: the licence key and renewal time. No renewal is required by the current alpha.
  • In your own shared workspace: authenticated account names, roles and operation audit records are stored on your service, not automatically sent to Granum.

Why

To verify downloads, answer pilot and support requests, and operate any separately agreed licensed access. Download verification does not start a subscription. We do not sell this data or send marketing email unless you ask; hosting and email providers process the requests as described below.

Where it is kept

Website records use our hosting provider (Vercel), database provider (Neon), and email provider. Contact us about retained download, pilot or legacy licensing records and deletion requests. Your configured shared-workspace records and browser drafts are managed on your systems.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, for it to be corrected, or for it to be deleted. Deleting a record can end separately licensed access tied to it; the current alpha has no enforced subscription countdown.
