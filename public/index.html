Product tour

One workbench for the whole loop, from raw labels to a trained model.

Most model problems are data problems. Granum makes your data visible, reviewable and versioned, so every training run uses data you have checked.

Catch problems when you import, not after training.

Point Granum at a COCO dataset. Before anything is imported, it checks every image and every box and tells you what it found and how many images are affected.

  • The same image, or frames of one sequence, in more than one split
  • Boxes with no width or height, and categories that share a name
  • Ignore regions and crowd labels recognised, not trained on as objects
  • Train, validation and test splits resized with a drag

Review every image. Correct any label.

Browse thousands of images with their labels, filter by class and status, and open any image full screen. Reviewers verify images, send them back for rework with a comment, or set them aside.

  • Draw and edit boxes, polygon masks and keypoints
  • Tools match the project type: detection, segmentation, keypoints or classification
  • Every save is a new version of the set. Nothing is overwritten.

Freeze what you train on, and see every augmentation first.

Create a named dataset version from the whole dataset or only its verified images. Add augmented copies of the training set, previewed on your own images before you commit.

  • Flip, rotate, crop, shear, colour, blur, noise and cutout
  • Boxes, masks and keypoints move with the image
  • Validation and test sets are never augmented

Train on a version. Score on a held-out test set.

Train YOLO, RT-DETR or RF-DETR on any dataset version. GPU support installs the first time you need it. Validation picks the best weights, and a separate test set is scored once, at the end.

  • Compare runs side by side on the same labels
  • All three model families scored the same way
  • Every run records exactly which data it trained on

See which images the model never learned.

Granum records the model's predictions on every image in each round. Images learned late, forgotten again or never learned point to labels worth a second look, and to classes that need more examples.

  • Every image sorted into learned early, late, unstable or never
  • Open any image to compare its labels with each round's predictions
  • Remove bad images into a new version, restorable at any time

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us and we will reply.

Does my data leave my computer?

No. Granum reads your images and labels where they are and keeps its work beside them. The app contacts our licence server only to sign in and renew. It sends your email, an ID for the computer (a hash, not a hardware serial number) and the app version.

What happens when the trial or a plan ends?

Granum becomes read-only. You can open every project, look at images and runs, and export. Creating, editing, importing and training resume when you buy or renew a plan.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. Sign in once, and the licence renews itself whenever the computer is online. It keeps working for up to 7 days without a connection.

What do I need to train models?

An NVIDIA GPU is strongly recommended. The training components (PyTorch with CUDA and the model libraries, a few gigabytes) download the first time you train. Everything else works without them.

Which formats can I import?

COCO datasets, the format Roboflow and most annotation tools export, with train, validation and test splits. You can also build projects from classes pulled out of your other projects.

Why does Windows say it protected my PC?

The installer is not yet signed with a commercial certificate, so Windows SmartScreen asks before running it. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
